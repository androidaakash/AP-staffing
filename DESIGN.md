## Brand & Style

This design system crafts an authoritative, enterprise-grade B2B presence tailored for global recruitment process outsourcing (RPO) and business process outsourcing (BPO). The visual identity balances elite international management consulting sophistication with operational precision and institutional reliability. 

The aesthetic is anchored in **Corporate / Modern** principles elevated by understated executive luxury:
- **Atmosphere:** Clean, calculated, and commanding. Ample whitespace conveys clarity of scale, while deep forest tones establish stability and institutional weight.
- **Audience:** Global C-suite executives, VP-level talent acquisition leaders, and procurement directors evaluating strategic offshore workforce solutions.
- **Emotional Resonance:** High trust, operational maturity, seamless global delivery, and rigorous governance.
- **Visual Tenets:** Razor-sharp typography hierarchy, structured multi-layer card surfaces, whisper-quiet borders, and purposeful amber accents reserved strictly for high-value points of conversion.

## Colors

The palette establishes an executive enterprise aura through deep botanical green anchored by architectural slate neutrals and deliberate warm amber highlights.

### Hierarchy & Functional Rules
- **Primary (`#0D3B2E`):** The institutional anchor. Used for primary navigation bars, primary action buttons, enterprise feature headers, and dominant corporate markers.
- **Secondary (`#1E293B`):** Deep warm slate providing high-contrast textual grounding and secondary structural modules like dark footer bands and metric panels.
- **Tertiary (`#D97706`):** Precision amber. Reserved strictly for tier-one call-to-actions, consultation booking triggers, key performance indicator highlights, and conversion pathways. It must never exceed 5% of visible canvas real estate.
- **Neutral (`#64748B`):** Governs structural borders (`#E2E8F0`), secondary supporting text (`#475569`), and subtle tinted backgrounds (`#F8FAFC`).
- **Surface Roles:** Base body canvas operates on `#FFFFFF` and `#F8FAFC`, maintaining maximum clean contrast against `#0F172A` text tokens.
- **Accessibility:** All primary, secondary, and tertiary pairings against their respective light or dark backgrounds strictly adhere to WCAG AA and AAA contrast thresholds (e.g., `#0D3B2E` on white delivers a 12.8:1 ratio).

## Typography

The typographic system utilizes **Manrope** for headlines and display layers paired with **Hanken Grotesk** for extended reading, UI components, and data grids.

- **Manrope:** Provides modern geometric equilibrium with subtle open curves, lending enterprise gravitas without the sterility of pure neo-grotesques. Display headings feature tight negative tracking for executive presence.
- **Hanken Grotesk:** Selected for clean neutral legibility across high-density recruitment metrics, process steps, case studies, and form interactions.
- **Hierarchy System:**
  - `label-caps` is styled in full uppercase to demarcate section categories, client tags, and stage markers.
  - Large display styles automatically collapse to mobile counterparts under 768px viewports to maintain proportional reading lines.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a disciplined 12-column fluid grid system bounded by a maximum container width of `1280px` (`80rem`) to guarantee structural composure on ultra-wide desktop monitors.

### Breakpoints & Column Framework
- **Mobile (`< 768px`):** 4-column layout; outer canvas margin `1rem`, column gutter `1rem`. Section vertical padding steps down to `space-2xl` (`4rem`).
- **Tablet (`768px – 1024px`):** 8-column layout; outer canvas margin `2rem`, column gutter `1.5rem`.
- **Desktop (`> 1024px`):** 12-column layout; outer canvas margin `3rem`, column gutter `2rem`. Section vertical padding scales to `space-3xl` (`6rem`).

### Rhythm & Alignment
Spacing follows an intentional 8pt spatial cadence. Structural section blocks prioritize expansive negative space (`space-2xl` to `space-3xl`) to project enterprise clarity, while internal card components maintain compact, high-density padding (`space-md` to `space-xl`) suited for operational metrics and complex capability lists.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids exaggerated neomorphic or gaming-oriented drop shadows in favor of **ambient architectural diffusion** combined with crisp, low-contrast structural outlines.

### Depth Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Surface white (`#FFFFFF`) or tinted slate (`#F8FAFC`). No shadow. Outlines used for separation (`1px solid #E2E8F0`).
- **Level 1 (Cards, Modules, Feeds):** Ambient executive shadow:
  - `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04);`
  - Paired with an inner border `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- **Level 2 (Hovered Cards, Dropdowns, Flyouts):**
  - `box-shadow: 0 10px 15px -3px rgba(13, 59, 46, 0.06), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03);`
  - Subtle upward translation of `translateY(-2px)` on interactive surfaces.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Executive Overlays):**
  - `box-shadow: 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05);`
  - Backdrop dimming uses an ultra-deep forest overlay: `rgba(13, 59, 46, 0.4)` with an optical blur of `4px`.

## Shapes

The system implements a geometric, disciplined roundedness profile (`roundedness: 2`):
- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Badges):** `0.5rem` (`8px`) corner radius for balanced tactility that conveys modern software without appearing toy-like.
- **Container Surfaces (Cards, Panels, Modals):** `1rem` (`16px`) corner radius (`rounded-lg`), providing soft, architectural enclosure.
- **Section Dividers & Metric Spotlights:** Sharp grid boundaries accented with precise 1px micro-dividers. Roundedness is explicitly rejected for full-width layout bands.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Institutional):** Solid background of `#0D3B2E`, text `#FFFFFF`, radius `0.5rem`, padding `0.75rem 1.5rem`. Hover state shifts to `#14532D`.
- **High-Impact Conversion (CTA):** Solid background `#D97706`, text `#FFFFFF`, weight 600. Reserved for consultation bookings and contact proposals. Hover state deepens to `#B45309`.
- **Secondary (Outlined):** Transparent background, `1.5px solid #0D3B2E`, text `#0D3B2E`. Hover transitions to background `rgba(13, 59, 46, 0.04)`.
- **Ghost:** Text `#334155`, hover background `#F1F5F9`.

### Cards & Case Studies
- **Structure:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, corner radius `1rem`, padding `space-xl`.
- **Interaction:** On hover, border shifts to `#CBD5E1` and elevation transitions to Level 2 with a `200ms ease-out` timing curve.
- **Metric Cards:** Feature an accent line or micro-tag in `#0D3B2E` with oversized Manrope data counters.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Badges:** Radius `0.375rem`, padding `0.25rem 0.625rem`, font `label-sm`.
- **Offshore / Domain Tags:** Tinted neutral `#F1F5F9`, text `#334155`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- **Verified / SLA Pill:** Tinted green `rgba(22, 163, 74, 0.1)`, text `#14532D`, border `1px solid rgba(22, 163, 74, 0.2)`.

### Form Inputs & Selects
- **Inputs:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, radius `0.5rem`, text `#0F172A`, placeholder `#94A3B8`, height `48px`.
- **Focus State:** Border changes to `#0D3B2E` with a focused halo: `0 0 0 3px rgba(13, 59, 46, 0.15)`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Checkboxes:** Square with `0.25rem` radius, border `1.5px solid #94A3B8`. Checked state renders solid `#0D3B2E` with a white checkmark icon.
- **Radio Buttons:** Concentric circles; checked state presents an inner active circle in `#0D3B2E`.

### Data Tables & Talent Pipeline Lists
- **Rows:** Alternating subtle zebra striping (`#FFFFFF` and `#F8FAFC`). Bottom border `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- **Header:** Background `#F8FAFC`, uppercase `label-caps` in `#64748B`, vertical padding `0.875rem`.
